4. Consider the birthday problem that in a room of n persons, what is the probability that 
two people share the same birthday. Create a simulation to solve this birthday problem. 
Observe the result of simulation with theoretical solution.  
 
The steps of simulation are as follows: 
 
i) Generate n random birthdays in the range 1 to 365 (ignore leap years). 
 
ii) Check if two coincide. 
 
iii) Repeat for different trials. 
 
iv) Probabilities =numbers of coincidences/numbers of trials.
